   Located in the historical and picturesque city of Hangzhou, Zhejiang University is a prestigious institution of higher education with a long history. Qiushi Academy, the predecessor of Zhejiang University, was founded in 1897 and was one of the earliest modern academies of higher learning established in China.  

 Zhejiang University focuses on assiduous study and research, and science and technological innovation. It has launched a number of international high-end academic platforms and gathered masters, scholars and high-level research teams in various disciplines. In recent years, Zhejiang University maintains a leading position in China in output indicators including publications, patents and etc., and has made abundant important achievements in science, technology, humanities and social sciences. Zhejiang University always takes initiatives in catering for national and regional needs, and exerts itself to become an influential source of innovation and a pool of talents.

 The School of Economics specializes in the following research fields: Finance, New Institutional Economics, Industrial Economics, Labor Economics, International Economics (International Trade and International Investment), Public Finance, Public Economics, and etc


Job Requirements

 We welcome applications at the ranks of Professor, Associate Professor, Assistant Professor, and Researcher. Applicants should hold a Ph.D. in Economics at the time of appointment. Specializations in the following fields: Finance, Labor Economics, Public Finance and Taxation, Accounting and Auditing，International Trade, Industrial Organization,Urban Economics, and Chinese Economy are preferred. Researchers on other fields are also welcomed. Candidates for the Professor and Associate Professor positions are expected to have a strong record of research publications.



Application Instructions

 We recruit from China International Conference in Economics（CICE）and American Social Science Association (ASSA)’s annual meetings every year. For fresh PhD graduates, we will highly recommend you to apply via either portal, application links can be found below (the portals will only be open during end-of-year recruiting seasons):

 For CICE: http://www.cicecon.org.cn/careers/detail.html?k=7D928B7B1C35099A

 For ASSA: https://www.aeaweb.org/joe/listing.php?JOE_ID=2019-02_111463617&

All the other applications will be reviewed regularly on a rolling basis. Please submit the required application materials to jjxy@zju.edu.cn for consideration.


Application materials

 Cover letter, CV, representative research paper (reference letters are not necessary at the moment of application, but should be ready upon request)
